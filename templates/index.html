The pipeline

The signal chain

Stage 01

Input

Your microphone is sampled in the browser and encoded to raw PCM — no compression, no lost detail.

Stage 02

Feature extraction

Python and librosa pull out pitch (F0), energy, spectral brightness, tempo, and 13 MFCCs — the features phoneticians use to describe a voice.

Stage 03

Affective scoring

Features are mapped onto arousal, valence, and tension, then compared against eight reference emotions.

Stage 04

Reading

The closest match becomes your dominant reading, shown alongside the full spread — not just a single guess.

Built on classical acoustic features mapped to affective dimensions by an explainable scoring model. Scores are relative shares across the eight classes, not calibrated probabilities. A trained classifier can be swapped in without changing the pipeline — see train_model.py.
